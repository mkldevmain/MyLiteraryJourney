Chapter IV · Introspection

Personal Reflection

A critical self-examination of academic hurdles, intellectual growth, and lifelong ethical takeaways.

1

How has your understanding and appreciation of literature changed throughout the course?

Rather than being a boring and tedious requirement, the classics form a huge discussion with people across generations. Great literature is not about how old it is or how scary it might be but how well it portrays humanity.

2

What challenges did you encounter when reading and analyzing literary masterpieces, and how did you overcome them?

The main difficulty I had was reading non-technical texts. This was difficult because the texts were focused on symbols and archaic language. The lengthy stories and complex metaphors were difficult to understand. I was able to overcome this by researching the background of each text, making notes of the key themes and discussing the texts with my classmates.

3

Which literary lens or approach influenced your interpretation of literature the most? Why?

The most impactful perspectives that were used were psychological and existential. As I am naturally quite curious as to why people make particular decisions when put under pressure it meant that the struggles of the characters with their internal conflict and finding meaning made all the stories very authentic.

4

What important realizations about life, society, culture, or human nature have you gained?

I noticed the main thing people struggle with is being popular and being themselves. Characters in the stories ended up negatively if they followed the toxic ideas without thinking and letting their ego get the better of them. They only found peace within themselves when they were humble. We are encouraged to do certain things by our society however, when we are actually being caring towards others we show who we really are as humans.

5

How can you apply the lessons and values learned from Great Books in your personal, academic, or future professional life?

I can use these lessons in my future. When I complete my IT studies and go into Cybersecurity I can apply the things that I have learned. Using technology to keep people safe, respect their privacy and create secure systems. I will use critical thinking, empathy and perspective in my daily life and career.
