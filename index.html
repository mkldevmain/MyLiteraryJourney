Brief Personal Introduction

Hello, I'm Michael Harvey C. Saturius (Mike), a fourth-year Bachelor of Science in Information Technology student persuing a specialization in Network Engineering and Information Assurance and Security. While much of my academic life revolves around configuring virtual environments, analyzing network traffic, and securing digital systems, I have a deep passion for literature and enjoy exploring different genres and perspectives through reading. I have always believed that technology is ultimately built to serve human stories. Stepping into the literary world allows me to balance the strict binary logic of computing with the nuanced, unpredictable nature of human emotion and philosophy a balance that first sparked my imagination long before I picked up traditional books.

Personal Interests & Experiences Related to Reading and Literature

My journey as a reader began through visual and interactive storytelling specifically anime series like Black Clover and narrative-driven video games. Today, my go-to reading materials are Manga and Manhwa, particularly in the Isekai, Comedy, RomCom, and Action genres, because they always provide a sense of thrill and excitement. What initially drew me into these mediums was the intricate world-building, character resilience, and the moral dilemmas faced by protagonists under pressure. Over time, that curiosity expanded into classic and contemporary literature, where I discovered that timeless epics, allegories, and modern short fiction explore the exact same human struggles for identity, justice, and meaning.

Expectations & Learning Experiences in the Great Books Course

When I first enrolled in Great Books, I expected a traditional survey of historical texts that might feel distant from modern life. However, this course made me realize that reading is not simply about going through sentences and paragraphs word for word it showed me that there is so much more to a text than what we usually see on the surface. By applying diverse literary lenses to works ranging from ancient epics to contemporary Philippine fiction, the course transformed how I read and interpret the world. I learned to dissect narratives much like I audit a complex IT system looking beneath the surface code to uncover cultural values, psychological motives, and timeless truths about the human condition.
