Chapter III · Synthesis

Summary of Learning

Core insights, analytical paradigms, and universal truths synthesized across the course.

1

Historical Periods & Cultures

I found that though stories have changed over the years the issues being addressed are still the same. Whether it be an old epic story such as The Ramayana, a classical Russian setting or modern Philippines story there was always a time capsule inside. Despite the advances in technology and changing times some aspects of life will stay the same such as love, honour and belonging.

2

Application of Literary Lenses

I would have previously considered stories purely in terms of the plot but using the various literary theories has helped me think about texts differently. Much like you would solve a problem within an IT system by looking at each layer, I have been able to look past the surface of stories to understand characters’ motivations and power struggles.

3

Literature & Human Experience

Throughout this course I have learned that books were never written in isolation. They are representative of society and show what is best and worst about cultures through their hypocrisies. Literature connects history with real human empathy.

4

Critical Thinking & Analysis

IT usually gives you only one way to do things whereas literature can give you many answers as long as you can prove IT using evidence from the text. This has made me a better thinker and communicator.
